## Issues Found
### Issue 1: Contact-form labels are too small
- **What**: The contact-form labels are styled at 9px on mobile and appear very small in the desktop form as well.
- **Where**: Contact slide, above the name, email, telephone, and message fields.
- **Why it matters**: Users may have difficulty scanning field labels, particularly on a phone or against the busy photographic background.
- **Suggested fix**: Increase labels to at least 12–13px on mobile and desktop, and preserve clear contrast against the translucent card.

### Issue 2: Desktop header still occupies substantial hero height
- **What**: The desktop header remains 112px high, leaving less vertical space for the opening photograph than the brief calls for.
- **Where**: Fixed header and home-hero image offset at desktop widths.
- **Why it matters**: The header uses about 12% of a 900px-tall viewport. The owners are visible in the inspected crop, but a little more photo height would better fulfill the request and protect faces on shorter screens.
- **Suggested fix**: Consider a modest further reduction, updating the logo/nav sizing and hero image offset together; retain the current mobile header treatment.

## Priority Fixes for Next Attempt
1. Enlarge contact-form labels for comfortable phone reading.
2. If space permits, trim the desktop header slightly and keep its logo and hero offset synchronized.
